# Track brief: The Intelligent FBO (NCBAA)

Text copy of the FBO section of `aviation-tracks-overview.pdf`, with the judging lens from `aviation-tracks-slides.pdf`. Received 2026-10-23.

## Mission
Build an AI-powered tool that helps an FBO at an LA-area airport prepare for demand during Super Bowl LXI week (game: Sun 2027-02-14, SoFi Stadium).

Choose one area. **Our choice: Parking. "When might demand exceed available aircraft parking space?"**

Choose one FBO or a fictional FBO at a real airport. Model a seven-day planning window around the game.

## The prototype must show
1. An ordinary-week baseline.
2. A forecast for Super Bowl week.
3. A recommended preparation plan.
4. How that plan changes under low, medium, and high demand.

## Data
- Public or synthetic data is allowed.
- Clearly identify assumptions about aircraft arrivals, fuel purchases, parking duration, and available resources.
- "Event attendance alone does not establish aircraft demand."

## Measure impact
Show forecast accuracy, or estimate improvements in resource availability, staffing coverage, parking capacity, or service delays.

## Humans in command
- Protect passenger privacy. No sensitive data.
- Explain uncertainty. Show assumptions.
- The tool supports planning. It does not authorize aircraft movements or replace airport safety procedures.
- Principle: "AI assists. People decide." "Recommendation ≠ decision."
- Be ready to answer: "What decision should your AI never be allowed to make?"

## Judging lens (from slides)
Innovation + accuracy / Practical preparation / Privacy, uncertainty and humans in command.

## Deliverables (from slides)
Working prototype (planning workflow), 3-minute pitch (user, problem, value), impact + disruption (baseline comparison and a demand spike or delay).

## Stretch goal
Adapt the solution to a selected period during the LA 2028 Olympics.
